# Project Brief: SBI PO AI Interview Coach

## Vision
A free, accessible, and high-quality web platform designed specifically for SBI PO (State Bank of India Probationary Officer) aspirants to practice realistic Phase-III interviews and receive structured AI evaluation.

## Target Audience
Banking exam aspirants across India preparing for the SBI PO interview round (especially candidates from small towns or without access to expensive private coaching institutes).

## Core Principles & Constraints
1. **100% Free**: No paywalls, subscription models, or hidden fees.
2. **Authentic Banking Focus**: Question patterns aligned with actual SBI interview panels (bio-data, banking awareness, RBI monetary policy, and situational judgment).
3. **Transparency**: Honest UI states; never fake AI responses or pretend evaluation has occurred before an API is integrated.
4. **Accessible & Responsive**: Fully usable across mobile phones, tablets, and desktops with high contrast and semantic HTML.
5. **Brand Identity**: Clean white background (`#ffffff`), deep navy blue primary elements (`#0a2540`), and subtle Indian financial saffron/amber accents (`#f59e0b` / `#d97706`).
